A QUICK GUIDE

Welcome to BizFlow

BizFlow helps you prepare clear business documents in a few steps.

  1. Open Business settings and add your business name.
  2. Select Create document and choose an invoice, quotation or receipt.
  3. Add your customer and line items. BizFlow calculates the total.
  4. Save the document, then print it or save it as a PDF.

Prototype note: documents are stored only in this browser on this device. Export or print important records and do not use this prototype as your only business record.
